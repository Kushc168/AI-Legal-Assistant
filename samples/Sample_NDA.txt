MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on 15 August 2026 between BlueRiver Analytics Private Limited ("BlueRiver") and Kestrel Partners LLP ("Kestrel").

SAMPLE DOCUMENT - fictitious parties, created for demonstration only.

1. PURPOSE

1.1 The parties wish to evaluate a possible joint venture for supply-chain analytics (the "Purpose") and, for that Purpose, may disclose confidential business information to each other.

2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

2.1 "Confidential Information" means any business, technical or financial information disclosed by either party, whether before or after the date of this Agreement, including pricing models, source code and client lists.

2.2 Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes public through no fault of the recipient, or that the recipient can show was already lawfully in its possession.

3. OBLIGATIONS

3.1 Each party shall use the other party's Confidential Information only for the Purpose and shall not disclose it to any person other than its employees and advisers who need to know it for the Purpose.

3.2 Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, and in any event no less than reasonable care.

4. NON-SOLICITATION

4.1 For a period of 12 months from the date of this Agreement, neither party shall solicit or employ any employee of the other party who was involved in the Purpose.

5. TERM

5.1 This Agreement remains in force for three (3) years from its date. Either party may end discussions about the Purpose at any time, but the obligations in Clause 3 survive for the full term.

6. RETURN OF INFORMATION

6.1 On written request, each party shall promptly return or destroy all Confidential Information of the other party and certify in writing that it has done so.

7. REMEDIES

7.1 If a party breaches Clause 3, it shall pay the other party liquidated damages of INR 50,00,000 for each breach, without prejudice to any other remedy available.

7.2 Each party acknowledges that damages alone may not be an adequate remedy and that the other party may seek injunctive relief.

8. GENERAL

8.1 Nothing in this Agreement obliges either party to enter into any further agreement or transaction.

8.2 This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both parties.
